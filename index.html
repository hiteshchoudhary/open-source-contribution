🚨 Important Notes

  • Start Low: Begin with 50% of recommended values and gradually increase over 2-3 weeks
  • Monitor Plants: Watch for signs of light stress (pale leaves, excessive pearling)
  • CO2 Balance: High light requires adequate CO2 supplementation (20-30 ppm)
  • Nutrient Demand: Intense lighting increases fertilizer requirements
  • Algae Prevention: Maintain proper photoperiod (8-10 hours max)

🎯 Why This Spectrum Works

This WRGB spectrum is scientifically optimized based on:

  • McCree Curve: Plants' photosynthetic response to different wavelengths
  • Chlorophyll Absorption: Peak efficiency at 430nm and 660nm
  • Emerson Effect: Enhanced photosynthesis with red + far-red combination
  • Underwater Attenuation: Optimized for light penetration in water
  • Morphological Control: Blue light prevents stretching and promotes bushiness
